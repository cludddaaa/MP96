Our Services

Personalized Individual Therapy

Individual therapy offers a private, confidential space to explore challenging emotions, thought patterns, and life events. Our licensed clinicians utilize a variety of **evidence-based modalities** tailored specifically to your goals and current needs.

Key Treatment Areas:

  • Managing and reducing symptoms of **Generalized Anxiety Disorder (GAD)**.
  • Treatment for **Depression** and low mood using CBT and behavioral activation.
  • Support through **Grief and Loss**, providing tools for healthy processing.
  • Developing self-compassion and improved **Self-Esteem**.
  • Coping strategies for high-stress professional environments and **Burnout**.
Start Individual Sessions

Modalities We Use

We are experts in blending techniques for optimal outcomes, including:

  • CBT: Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for modifying negative thinking patterns.
  • DBT: Dialectical Behavior Therapy focusing on emotional regulation and distress tolerance.
  • Trauma-Informed Care: Creating a safe environment focused on healing from past experiences.

Focus on Connection

We guide partners and families to move past gridlock and find common ground:

  • Gottman Method: Used to predict and prevent relational breakdown by building sound relationship houses.
  • EFT: Emotionally Focused Therapy to understand and shift the underlying emotional distress in the relationship.
  • Family Systems: Analyzing the family unit to address issues affecting children or adolescents.
Our Services

Couples and Relationship Support

Relationships are complex. Our counseling services are designed to facilitate effective and empathetic dialogue, transforming conflict into deeper understanding and intimacy. We work with couples, pre-marital partners, and families of all structures.

We Address Issues Including:

  • Persistent **Communication Barriers** and misunderstandings.
  • Healing after **Infidelity** or loss of trust.
  • Navigating **Major Life Transitions** (e.g., parenthood, retirement).
  • Improving co-parenting and blended **Family Dynamics**.

Our Services

Group Therapy and Corporate Wellness

Group work provides the unique benefit of shared experience, reducing feelings of isolation and allowing for peer support under the guidance of a clinician. Our corporate workshops bring these vital skills directly into the workplace.

Current Offerings:

  • **Mindfulness for Anxiety:** Weekly open group focused on meditation and breathwork.
  • **Resilience Training:** A 6-week structured course for building emotional fortitude.
  • **Workplace Stress Reduction:** On-site or remote workshops for teams and managers.
  • **Support Groups:** Ongoing peer support for specific concerns (e.g., new parents, chronic illness).
Inquire About Group Enrollment

Benefits of Group Support

Joining a group or workshop can rapidly accelerate your progress by:

  • Community: Realizing you are not alone in your struggle.
  • New Perspectives: Gaining insights from diverse experiences.
  • Skill Practice: Practicing new behaviors and communication skills in a safe setting.
